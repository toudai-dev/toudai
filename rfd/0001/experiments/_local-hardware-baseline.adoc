== Local Hardware Baseline

- Experiment storage path: {benchmark-storage-context}
- CPU: AMD Ryzen 5 3600, one socket and NUMA node, 6 physical cores / 12 SMT
  threads, 32 MiB L3, with the host reporting a 0.558–4.208 GHz frequency
  range.
- RAM: 4 × 16 GiB DDR4 DIMMs (62.7 GiB OS-visible), configured at 2,133 MT/s;
  69.0 GiB swap was present. No memory-bandwidth benchmark was captured.
- OS disk: Samsung SSD 980 1 TB NVMe, LUKS-backed ext4.
- Hot SSD tier before Experiment 010: Samsung 870 QVO 1 TB plus Samsung 870
  EVO 1 TB in a ZFS mirror, 900 GiB usable. Its throughput was not measured
  before the pool was replaced, so no later striped result is attributed to it.
- Hot SSD tier from Experiment 010 onward: the same two SATA SSDs as independent
  top-level ZFS vdevs, 1.81 TiB usable, with no redundancy.
- Bulk/source tier: 2 × Seagate ST4000DM004 4 TB HDDs as independent top-level
  ZFS vdevs, 7.25 TiB usable, with no redundancy.

The following storage speed baseline was captured on 2026-08-30, after the
historical experiments, with `fio` 3.41. Each tier used a temporary 8 GiB file,
direct I/O, one job, 1 MiB sequential I/O at queue depth 8, and 4 KiB random
reads for 30 seconds at queue depth 32. Sequential writes included
`end_fsync=1`. These measurements characterize the current host and filesystem
state; they are not retroactive measurements of a historical run. The hot pool
was 79% allocated and the bulk pool was 74% allocated when captured.
