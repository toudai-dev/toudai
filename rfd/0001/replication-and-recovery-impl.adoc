== PoC-required: small-topology recovery

Depends on the durable vertical slice and raw-query correctness. Topology and
failure assumptions must be written before execution; this test does not require
a 100 TB corpus or establish cross-zone production resilience.

* [ ] Run the deterministic fixture on a small replicated multi-shard topology; stop a replica, restore it, and verify the declared availability/completeness behavior and data convergence.
* [ ] Rebuild a serving replica and restart the coordinator while querying the fixture; verify no silently incomplete successful responses and recovery within a prospectively declared time budget.
